02 • Logo System

Logo & Brand Mark

The logo must remain legible and recognisable across light and dark backgrounds, small app icons, and social previews.

The “Bump It Along” logo is built around a modern, minimal interpretation of the letter B, cleverly shaped to evoke the silhouette of a baby carriage. This dual‑meaning form creates an immediate connection to maternity and early parenthood while still feeling fresh, lifestyle‑driven, and not overly baby‑centric.

Primary logo

Use the full logo for navigation bars, hero sections, and social sharing images. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the brand mark around all sides.

Logo variants

• Full logo
• Icon / brand mark
• Monochrome version
• White version
• Black version
• Transparent PNGs
• Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG)

Icon & favicon

The icon should be a simplified mark that remains recognisable at very small sizes. Avoid thin details that disappear on low-resolution screens.

Login / hero logo placement

On login or sign-up screens, place the logo in the upper area with sufficient negative space. Ensure contrast against the background image and avoid placing forms directly over the logo.

In essence

The logo captures the spirit of parenthood — moving forward, sharing, growing, and passing things along — all wrapped into a clean, modern lifestyle mark that feels fresh, warm, and unmistakably yours.

03 • Logo Concept Overview

Concept Behind the Logo

The logo blends maternity symbolism with modern lifestyle aesthetics, using the letter “B” as a clever dual‑meaning icon.

Designed to feel warm, minimal, and community‑driven, the logo represents the journey of parenthood and the circular nature of resale.

1. The Letter B as a Baby Carriage

The rounded geometry of the “B” mirrors the soft curves of a baby carriage, creating an immediate connection to maternity without relying on cliché baby icons.

2. Motion & Momentum

Integrated wheels and motion lines suggest movement, wind, and forward progress — symbolizing the journey of parenthood and the flow of community.

3. The “Bumpy Road” Metaphor

The carriage travels along an invisible, lightly uneven path, representing the real, imperfect, joyful bumps of raising a child.

4. Lifestyle First, Baby Second

Clean lines and modern simplicity position the brand as a lifestyle community rather than a purely baby‑focused store.

5. Symbol + Wordmark Balance

The icon stands confidently on its own for avatars and tags, while the refined wordmark ensures a cohesive identity across all brand uses.

Core Colors

The palette balances soft, nurturing tones with a clear accent for calls-to-action and marketplace highlights.

Use the primary accent for buttons and key highlights, neutrals for backgrounds, and soft supporting tones for cards and badges.

Suggested UI color usage

• Background: deep neutral or soft gradient
• Cards: semi-transparent glass surfaces
• Primary buttons: primary accent with glass effect
• Secondary buttons: neutral glass with accent border
• Error states: warm red accent (e.g. #ff4f6a)

Accessibility

Ensure contrast ratios meet WCAG AA for body text and key UI elements. Test primary accent against both light and dark backgrounds and adjust saturation if needed.

05 • Typography

Type System

Typography should feel calm and approachable, with clear hierarchy for marketplace listings, prices, and CTAs.

Recommended: a rounded or humanist sans-serif for body text, and a slightly more condensed display style for headings.

Accent / numbers
123 • Prices
Use a clear numeric style for prices and quantities. Avoid overly decorative fonts.
Hierarchy

• H1: 32–40px, uppercase or small caps for key pages
• H2: 24–30px, used for section titles
• H3: 18–20px, used for card titles
• Body: 14–16px, line-height 1.5–1.7
• Microcopy: 11–12px for labels and helper text

Do / Don’t

Do keep spacing generous and legible.
Do maintain consistent letter-spacing for uppercase labels.
Don’t mix more than two font families.
Don’t use script fonts for core UI elements.

06 • Brand Pattern System

Logo‑Derived Pattern

The brand pattern is created from the logo’s soft curves and circular maternity‑inspired geometry. It is used for marketing, packaging, and storytelling — not UI components.

The pattern should always feel soft, airy, and minimal. Use generous spacing and pastel tones from the brand palette. Avoid heavy contrast or dense repetition.

Soft Scatter Pattern
○ ○ ○
Airy placement of shapes. Ideal for backgrounds and social media.
Structured Grid Pattern
□ □ □
Even spacing and alignment. Best for packaging and printed materials.
Circular Flow Pattern
◜ ◝ ◟
Shapes arranged along a loop. Great for hero banners and dividers.
Hamburger Icon Pattern
≡ ≡ ≡
This pattern create a cohesive, modern, lifestyle‑friendly pattern system.
Usage Guidelines

• Use behind content, never in front
• Opacity 6%–18% for subtlety
• Avoid placing shapes too close to the logo
• No distortion or aggressive rotation
• Do not use inside UI elements (buttons, cards, nav)
• Keep spacing generous and breathable

Color Rules

• Use pastel tones from the brand palette
• Accent colors sparingly
• Avoid dark backgrounds unless opacity is reduced
• Shapes should use solid fills (no gradients)

Brand Applications

• Social media backgrounds
• Printed brochures
• Marketplace promotional banners
• Packaging concepts
• Brand merchandise
• Section dividers in documents

08 • Logo Usage

Correct & Incorrect Use

Consistent logo usage protects brand recognition and ensures a professional appearance across all touchpoints.

Show side-by-side examples of correct and incorrect usage for quick reference.

Correct usage

• Maintain clear space around the logo.
• Use approved color variants only.
• Place logo on backgrounds with sufficient contrast.
• Scale proportionally; do not stretch or skew.
• Use icon-only version for small spaces (e.g. app icon).

Incorrect usage

• Do not change logo colors arbitrarily.
• Do not add outlines, shadows, or effects not specified.
• Do not place logo over busy imagery without contrast.
• Do not rotate, distort, or crop the logo.
• Do not use unapproved fonts alongside the logo lockup.
